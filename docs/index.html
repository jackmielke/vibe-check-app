Common questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No. Scoring happens on your device and the image is never sent to a server.
How do I delete something I shared?
Open the feed, tap the ••• on your own post, and choose Delete.
Someone posted something inappropriate.
Tap the ••• on that post and choose Report, or Block to stop seeing them. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
Can I change how the app looks?
Yes — pick Field, Real, Neo, or Meme from the theme row on the home screen.
The feed says it is offline.
Check your connection and reopen the app. If it persists, email us.

Privacy Policy

Vibe — last updated August 2026

The short version

Your photos never leave your phone. Vibe scores a selfie entirely on your device and uploads nothing about the image itself. We ask for no name, no email, and no password, and we run no analytics or advertising code.

Photos

When you take or choose a photo, Vibe reads it locally to produce a score. The image is not uploaded, stored on a server, or shared with any third party. It stays in your camera roll and in the app's memory for the length of the session.

What we store, and only if you tap Share

Sharing a result to the public feed saves:

  • the numeric score (0–100)
  • the generated description of the result
  • the five factor values behind the score
  • a randomly generated display name, such as “golden fern”
  • an anonymous account identifier and a timestamp

The anonymous identifier is created by Vibe and is not linked to your Apple ID, your phone number, or any contact detail. If you never tap Share, nothing about you is stored.

Reporting and blocking

Reporting a post records which post was reported, the reason chosen, and the anonymous identifier of the reporter, so we can review it. Blocking someone records the two anonymous identifiers involved so the feed can hide their posts from you. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and content that breaks the rules is removed.

Deleting your data

Every post you make can be deleted from inside the app: open the feed, tap the ••• on your own post, and choose Delete. To remove everything at once, email the address below from the device and we will erase the anonymous account and its posts.

Service providers

The feed is hosted on Supabase, and app updates are delivered by Expo. They process data only to provide those functions.

Children

Vibe is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top will change and the new version will appear at this address.
